# Inbox staff guide

For Marketing and Operations staff who work conversations every day, and anyone with view-only Inbox access. The full
reference is the [Inbox usage guide](../usages/inbox/usage-guide.md); this page is the short version.

> **Status:** several features below are merged or built but not yet proven in a live signed-in browser. The
> [checklist](checklist.md) says which. If something here does not appear for you, your agency may not have it switched on
> yet; ask an administrator.

## Your day

1. Open **Assigned to me**, then clear **Overdue**, **Payments**, **Complaints** and **Documents** before routine enquiries.
2. Work **Needs a reply** from the oldest waiting customer.
3. Reply, add an internal note, or hand over. A note is never sent to the customer.
4. Before you finish, check that nothing you own is **Overdue**.

## Keyboard shortcuts

Press **?** in the Inbox to see them all. They never act while you are typing.

| Key | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| `j` / `k` | Next / previous conversation |
| `/` | Search conversations |
| `r` | Reply to the customer |
| `n` | Add an internal note |
| `a` | Assign an owner |
| `q` | Start a **draft** quote (nothing is sent) |
| `e` / `b` | Open the linked lead / booking |
| `g` then `d` | Open the linked departure group |
| `Esc` | Close the open panel |

A shortcut only works if the matching button is visible and available to you. If you cannot do the thing with the mouse,
the key will not do it either.

## Voice notes

Voice notes show a **Staff only** transcript when your agency has it on. It is a machine aid: it can be wrong, and it is
never sent to the customer. A **Low confidence** badge means listen to the audio before you act on the words. Marketing,
Operations, Finance, Visa, CEO and Admin can read transcripts.

## Files customers send

- A brochure or ordinary file can be saved to **Documents** (if your role manages Documents). It is a copy for staff;
  nothing goes to the customer.
- **Passports and receipts are never saved to the shared Documents area.** Passports follow the passport review; receipts
  go to Finance as evidence. The Save button simply will not offer them.

## Marking spam

Select one or more conversations and choose **Mark as spam**, then confirm. It is reversible: open the **Spam** queue,
select them and choose **Restore from spam**.

- A chat is **not** marked if its lead has a booking or it has an open review. It is skipped and you are told why.
- If any selected chat is not your agency's, nothing is changed.
- Spam marking does not delete anything, and it does not stop the WhatsApp assistant from answering a message that
  arrives later.
- A chat whose **lead** is marked spam cannot be restored here; change the lead's stage in Leads first.

## Things you must not do

- Do not promise a price, seat, refund, visa outcome, discount or bank detail that is not in the CRM or approved by the
  owner of that topic.
- Do not copy a low-confidence Copilot fact into the CRM without checking it.
- Do not treat a customer's "I have paid" as payment. Finance confirms payments.

## If something goes wrong

| Problem | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| A message shows **Failed** | Choose **Retry**. It will not send twice. |
| You marked the wrong chats as spam | Open **Spam**, select them, **Restore from spam**. |
| Two people are replying | Check the presence warning, then agree who owns it using **Assign owner**. |
| A button is missing | Your role may not allow it. Ask an administrator rather than looking for a workaround. |
| A number on the Outcomes panel says **Could not be read** | It is not zero. Try again in a moment, and tell an administrator if it stays. |

## What good looks like

Nothing overdue under your name at the end of the day, every payment or document matter handed to the right person, and no
commitment made that the CRM does not support. Managers can see counts in **Outcomes**; each count has an **Open these
conversations** button so you can check the same list they are looking at.
