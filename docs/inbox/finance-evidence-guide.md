# Finance evidence guide

For Finance, Admin and the CEO. A customer's payment screenshot or receipt is **evidence to review, never a payment**.
Nothing here creates, changes or confirms a payment until a person decides.

## Who can do what

| Role | See pending receipts | Match to a payment or dismiss |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Finance | Yes | Yes |
| Admin | Yes | Yes |
| CEO | Yes | **No** (view only) |
| Everyone else | No | No |

The server checks this every time; a hidden button is not the only barrier.

## Daily routine

1. Open **Finance → Payments** and the receipt evidence list.
2. For each receipt, read the suggested payments and **why** each was suggested:
   - the receipt reference matches the payment's reference;
   - the receipt amount equals the payment's amount;
   - the receipt's conversation is linked to the same booking or departure group;
   - the payment was recorded on the same day, or within seven days.
3. **Match to this payment** only when you have checked the amount and reference yourself against the bank record.
4. If it is not proof of payment, choose **Dismiss receipt** and enter a reason. Dismissing creates and changes no payment.

The suggestions are ranked by these reasons only. The system never applies a match on its own, never matches on a close
but different amount, and never matches across agencies.

## Boundaries

- A receipt never goes to the shared Documents area; it comes here.
- A customer saying "I have paid" in the Inbox is a **Payments** queue item to check, not a confirmation.
- Do not tell a customer a payment is received until you have matched it and the payment record shows it.
- Bank details are only sent from the approved list.

## Recovery

| Situation | Action |
| --- | --- |
| Matched to the wrong payment | There is no undo for a match in the Inbox. Tell an Admin; correct the payment through the ledger and note why. Do not re-match the same receipt. |
| Dismissed by mistake | A decided receipt cannot be re-opened from the screen. The dismissal and reason are kept; ask the customer to send the receipt again, and record the payment from the new one. |
| Two people open the same receipt | The first decision wins; the second is told another reviewer has already decided it. |
| No suggestion appears | Nothing matched on reference or amount. Check the payment manually, or dismiss with a reason. |

## Expected outcomes

Every receipt has a decision and a reason trail; the **Payments** queue shrinks; and the **Median time to finish a payment
review** and **Slowest 5% of payment reviews** cards (Finance, Admin and CEO see them in **Outcomes**) show how quickly
reviews are closed. Those two figures are for the last 30 days and show **No data yet** until a review has been resolved.
