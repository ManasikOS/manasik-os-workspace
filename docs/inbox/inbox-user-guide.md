# PART 1 — UNDERSTAND IT

## 1. What is the Inbox?

Customers write to us on **WhatsApp, Facebook Messenger, Instagram and email**. Without the Inbox you would have to open four different apps all day.

**The Inbox puts every customer message in one place.** From there you can:

| You can… | Example |
|---|---|
| **Read** every message | A customer asks about Umrah in March. |
| **Reply** | You answer, and the customer gets it on the same app they wrote on. |
| **Send files** | Send a brochure or a PDF form. |
| **Leave private notes** | “Finance, please check this slip.” The customer never sees notes. |
| **Share the work** | Give a chat to a colleague. |
| **Connect to the CRM** | See the customer's lead, booking, payments balance and trip from the same screen. |
| **Get help from Copilot** | Copilot reads the chat and *suggests* what to do and what to write. |

**Think of it as a shared shop counter.** Everyone can see who is waiting, who is serving them, and what has already been said.

### Copilot: helpful, but you are in charge

**Copilot** is the helper built into the Inbox. It can read a conversation, work out what the customer wants, find a matching trip and suggest a reply. In the Inbox screen **it only suggests. You decide, and you press Send.**

> **Copilot can be wrong.** The CRM (the leads, bookings and payments pages) is the truth. Never promise a price, a seat, a payment, a visa result or a refund unless the CRM or the right team confirms it.

(Your administrator can switch on limited *automatic* replies for simple things like “Hello, how can we help?”. Even then, money, visa, booking, refund, health and religious questions always go to a person.)

## 2. Words you will see

| Word | What it means in plain English |
|---|---|
| **Conversation / chat** | All the messages with one customer on one app. |
| **Queue** | A ready-made list, like “Needs a reply”. The system fills it for you. A chat can be in several queues at once. |
| **Owner** | The one colleague responsible for a chat. You see “Assigned to you”, “Assigned to Nadeesha” or “Unassigned”. |
| **Assistant / Copilot** | The automatic helper. “Assistant active” = it may answer. “Assistant paused” = a person owns the chat and the helper stays quiet. |
| **Internal note** | A private message for the team. **The customer never sees it.** |
| **Reply window** | WhatsApp, Messenger and Instagram only let you type freely for **24 hours** after the customer's last message. |
| **Template** | A WhatsApp message pre-approved by Meta. It is the only way to start a WhatsApp chat or to message after the 24 hours. **Meta may charge for it.** |
| **Lead** | The customer's sales record in the CRM. |
| **Departure group** | A scheduled trip (for example “Umrah – March”) with seats and a price. |
| **Human review required** | A red card meaning “a person must look at this before going further”. |
| **Blocking review** | A review marked **“Do not confirm yet”**. While it is open you cannot send words that confirm the thing it protects (for example “payment received”). |
| **Spam** | Junk chats moved out of your way. Nothing is deleted, and you can restore them. |

| # | Area | What it is for |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Queues** (left menu) | Pick *which list* you want. Numbers show how many chats are in each. |

### The queues (the left menu)

**You do not move chats into queues. The system does it for you**, and moves them as things change.

**Always visible — your daily lists**

| Queue | A chat is here when… | What you do |
|---|---|---|
| **All conversations** | It is open | A wide look. |
| **Assigned to me** | **You** own it | Your personal to-do list. **Start here.** |
| **Unassigned** | Nobody owns it | Take it or give it to someone. |
| **Needs a reply** | The customer wrote last | **Reply.** This is the main working list. |
| **Waiting for customer** | We replied last | Nothing to do unless they have been quiet a long time. |
| **Waiting for our team** | A colleague was asked to help, or a review is open | Follow up with that colleague. |

**Shown when they have chats**

| Group | Queue | A chat is here when… |
|---|---|---|
| Inbox | **Closed**, **Spam** | Finished / junk. |
| **Sales** | **New enquiries** | A first-time customer asks about a trip. |

| | **Ready to book** | The customer agreed and only needs a booking. |
| | **Quote sent** | A quote is with the customer. |
| **Needs attention** | **Urgent** | A blocking review is open, or the customer needs urgent help. |
| | **Complaints** | The customer is unhappy or wants a refund. |
| | **Payments** | The customer talks about paying, or a payment claim needs checking. |
| | **Documents** | Passports, photos or other documents are involved. |
| | **Visa questions** | The customer asks about a visa. |
| | **Nearing deadline** | A reply target or messaging window is about to run out. |

If your agency uses the **simple menu**, you will only see All, Assigned to me, Unassigned, the four apps, Closed and Spam. Everything else in this guide still applies.

### The screen adapts to its size

| Your screen | What you get |
|---|---|
| Wide | All four areas. |
| Laptop | Queue menu folds to icons (click **Expand menu** to open it). |
| Small laptop / tablet | Customer details open in a side sheet (button in the chat header). |
| Phone | A bar above the list replaces the menu. You see **either** the list **or** one chat. Tap **All chats** to go back to the list. |

## 4. The golden rules

**Always**

1. ✅ Read the **banner above the reply box** and any **red review card** *before* you type.
2. ✅ Check the **Reply / Internal note** tab before you press Send.
3. ✅ Check **price, currency, what is included and live seats** before sending anything Copilot drafted.
4. ✅ Leave a **note** (and mention the person) whenever a colleague must act.
5. ✅ Confirm names, numbers and dates from **voice notes in writing**.
6. ✅ Ask Finance before saying a payment arrived.
7. ✅ Use **Retry** once for a failed message — never copy and paste it again.
8. ✅ **Search first** before starting a new chat — the person may already have one.

**Never**

1. ❌ Promise a price, seat, discount, refund, visa result, bank detail or religious ruling that the CRM or the right team has not confirmed.
2. ❌ Treat “I have paid” or a payment slip as money received. **Only Finance confirms payments.**
3. ❌ Copy something Copilot is unsure about into the CRM without checking the original message.
4. ❌ Forward or repeat passport or bank details in a chat.
5. ❌ Delete a conversation unless you are certain. **It cannot be undone.**
6. ❌ Bulk-close chats you have not looked at. There is no undo button.

# PART 2 — YOUR FIRST DAY

## 5. Opening the Inbox

1. Click the **Inbox icon** in the top bar of the CRM.
2. The Inbox opens in a **new browser tab**, full screen.
3. If you open a link someone sent you (it ends with `?conversation=…`), it opens straight on that chat.
4. Clicking a number on the CRM dashboard's **Inbox panel** opens the matching queue.
5. To leave, click **Back to the CRM** (bottom of the left menu, or the arrow at the top on a small screen).

**Good to know**
- On a computer, the **first chat in the list opens by itself** and is marked **read**.
- If the page does not open at all, your role (the *Guide* role) does not include the Inbox.
- If your agency account is suspended, you will see a “suspended” page instead.
- New messages, new chats, owner changes and tick marks appear **by themselves**. You never need to refresh. If you lose internet, it catches up when you are back.

## 6. Your daily routine

A simple, safe order for the day:

| When | Do this |
|---|---|
| **Start of day** | 1. **Assigned to me** → clear what is waiting. 2. **Urgent**. 3. **Complaints**. 4. **Payments**. 5. **Overdue**. |
| **Morning** | **Documents**, then **Needs a reply** (oldest first). |
| **During the day** | Glance at **Nearing deadline** now and then — those chats are about to become overdue. |
| **Team leads** | **Unassigned** — give every chat an owner. |
| **End of day** | Check **Assigned to me**, **Urgent**, **Payments**, **Overdue**. Leave notes on anything unfinished. Close finished chats. |

**Going on leave or ending a shift?** Hand your chats over — see scenario [Q](#q-you-are-going-on-leave-or-ending-your-shift).

# PART 3 — HOW DO I…?

## 7. Find a conversation

**Search**
1. Click **Search conversations** (or press **/**).
2. Type **at least 2 characters**: a **name**, **phone number**, **email**, **lead reference** (like LD-1042) or **package name**. Sinhala, Tamil and Arabic names work.
3. The list filters at once. A moment later it also searches **all** conversations, not just those on screen, under **“Matches from all conversations”** (at most 50 shown — type more letters to narrow it down).
4. Symbols are ignored. If it says **Search failed**, click **Try again**.

**Peek without opening** — hold your mouse over a row to see who owns it, its status, the lead, the package, whether the reply window is open, who spoke last and more.

**Save a favourite view** (the **bookmark** button)
1. Pick a queue and/or type a search.
2. Click the bookmark → **Save this view** → give it a name (up to 40 letters) → **Save**.
3. Next time, click the bookmark and choose it. Remove one with the **×**.

**Load more** — at the bottom of a long list, click **Load older chats**.

**What a row shows**: a coloured circle with initials (always the same colour for that customer) with a tiny app logo; the name (**bold** = unread messages); how long ago; a preview (“**You:**” means your side spoke last; media shows “Photo”, “Voice message”…); and a number badge for unread messages.

## 8. Read a conversation

Click a chat. It opens and its unread number clears.

| What you see | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Left, white bubbles** | The customer. |
| **Right, tinted bubbles** | Our team. |
| **Outlined bubbles with ✨ “Copilot”** | A message the automatic assistant sent. |
| **Dashed note cards** | **Internal notes.** The customer cannot see these. |
| **Small italic centre text** | System messages. |
| **Day dividers** | “Today”, “Yesterday”, dates. |

**Ticks under messages we sent**

| You see | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Sending…** | On its way. |
| **Sent** | Left the CRM. |
| **Delivered** | Reached the customer's phone or inbox. |
| **Read** | The customer opened it. |
| **Failed** | It did **not** reach them. A red line explains why. |

**Scrolling up to read old messages?** If a new message arrives, the screen does not jump. A button appears — **“1 new message”**. Click it to go down.

**Header** (top of the chat): name, phone or email, the **owner**, a **status chip**, the **⋯** menu, and a **panel button** to show or hide customer details.

## 9. Reply to a customer

The box at the bottom has two tabs: **Reply** (goes to the customer) and **Internal note** (team only).

> **Check the tab before pressing Send.**

**To reply**
1. Click **Reply** (or press **r**).
2. Type your message.
3. Press **Enter** (or the send arrow). **Shift + Enter** starts a new line.
   - If you type Sinhala, Tamil or Arabic and the keyboard is still suggesting a word, **Enter picks the word and does not send.**
4. Your message appears at once as **Sending…**, then **Sent / Delivered / Read**.

**What happens behind the scenes**
- If the helper or nobody had the chat, **sending makes you the owner** and pauses the helper. You will see a warning first.
- If a **colleague** already owns it, your message still goes out and **they stay the owner** — but you are warned: *“Check with them before you reply so the customer gets one answer.”*
- The system checks the rules **again at the moment you press Send**, whatever the screen shows.

**Why is the box greyed out?** The notice above it tells you. Usually: the chat is **closed**; your role cannot reply; the **24-hour window** closed (see [Part 5](#21-whatsapp-messenger-instagram-and-email-what-you-may-send)); a Messenger/Instagram customer has not written yet; or that app cannot send replies yet. **Internal notes still work.**

**Saved replies** — click the **speech-bubble** button, then a ready-made answer. It is **added to your box** so you can edit it. Roles that are allowed can add a new one with **+ Create reply**.

**Your draft is saved automatically** — click away or close the tab and your half-written reply is still there. Drafts are private to you.

**“Someone is writing”** — if a colleague is typing in the same chat you see *“Name is writing a reply. Please coordinate before sending.”* It is a **warning, not a lock**. Add a note (“I'll take this”) so you do not both answer.

**If sending fails** — the message stays with **“Not sent”** and **Retry / Dismiss**. **Retry is safe**: if the first try actually worked, the customer will *not* get it twice.

**Email replies** also show a **Subject** (pre-filled “Re: …”) and **Add Cc/Bcc**. Email has no 24-hour limit.

## 10. Send or receive files, photos and voice notes

### Sending a file to a customer

1. Click **+** (**Attach a file**) → **Choose from device** or **Choose from vault** (your shared Document Vault — good for brochures).
2. A chip shows the name and size. **×** removes it.
3. Add a caption if you like (optional).
4. Send.

| Type | Allowed | Biggest |
|---|---|---|
| **Photos** | JPG, JPEG, PNG | 5 MB |
| **Documents** | PDF, Word (.docx), Excel (.xlsx), PowerPoint (.pptx) | 10 MB |

- **One file per message.**
- **Instagram accepts photos only.** WhatsApp, Messenger and Email accept photos and documents.
- The system checks what is *inside* the file. Office files with **macros** and PDFs with **scripts** are refused.
- If the chip turns red it tells you why — remove it and try again.

### Files customers send you

| The customer sends… | What you see |
|---|---|
| **Photo** | A picture. Click to open full size. |
| **Document** | A link with the file name. |
| **Voice message** | A small player: **Play/Pause**, a progress bar and a **speed** button. Only one plays at a time. |
| **Video, location, contact card, sticker** | **Nothing.** These are not accepted. The customer automatically gets a polite message asking for text, an image, a document or a voice message. |

If you see **“Loading photo…”** for more than a few minutes, the file may have failed or expired — ask the customer to send it again.

**Voice transcript** (if your agency has switched it on): a **“Staff only”** box shows the machine-typed words. **It can be wrong. Listen to the audio**, and confirm any names, numbers, dates or amounts in writing. The customer never sees the transcript.

### Passport photo or PDF — “Attachment review”

A card lists the fields the system **read** (number, expiry, name…) and how sure it is. Fields under **90%** are outlined in red: **“Check this field”**.

1. **Open the original** and compare it with the fields. Never trust the fields alone.
2. If it asks, choose the right **Traveller**.
3. **Review fields** → correct the number and expiry → **Save to traveller record**. *Only your confirmation writes to the traveller's record.* (Administrator, Operations, Visa)
4. **Save to Documents** — copies the passport into the traveller's checklist (it becomes *submitted*, not *verified*). (Administrator, Operations, Visa)
5. **Assign visa officer** if needed.
6. If a **“passport may not be valid long enough”** review appears, ask for a renewed passport. Resolve the review with a note.

If you do not save it, the Inbox copy **expires** on the date shown. **Passports are never saved to the shared Document Vault.**

### Payment slip or receipt — “Attachment review”

The card shows a possible **amount, reference and date** and the label **“Not verified”** — *a payment counts only after Finance has checked it.*

- **Send acknowledgement** — puts a polite “we received your proof; our finance team will check it” message in your box. You read, edit and send. It does **not** confirm payment.
- **Copy to Finance** / **Open Finance review** — for **Administrator, CEO and Finance** only. If you are Marketing or Operations, write an **internal note and mention Finance** instead.

### Brochure or other file

The card says **“Choose where to keep this file. Nothing is sent to the customer.”** Options: **Save as proposal collateral** (brochure), **Save to Documents** (shared vault), or **Download the original**. The file must be PDF, JPG, PNG, Word, Excel or PowerPoint, up to 10 MB.

## 11. Leave a private note for a colleague

1. Click **Internal note** (or press **n**).
2. Type the note.
3. To get someone's attention, click **Mention staff** and pick a name (up to 20). They are **notified**.
4. Click **Add note**.

Notes work even on **closed** chats and when replying is blocked. **The customer never sees them.**

## 12. Start a new conversation

Click **+** at the top of the list. Choose **WhatsApp chat** or **Email**. (These only appear if your role can send messages.)

> **Search first.** If that number or email already has a chat, the system reuses it. If a **colleague owns it**, you are told who, and **nothing is sent**.

### New WhatsApp chat

1. Type the **WhatsApp number with country code**, digits only, 8–15 digits (for example `94771234567`). No `+`, no leading `0`.
2. A line tells you what will happen: *“linked to Amina (LD-1042)”*, or *no lead uses this number yet*, or *more than one lead uses it, so it will not be linked*.
3. **Contact name** is optional.
4. Choose an **approved template**. If none: *“No approved templates are available…”* — your administrator must sync them.
5. Fill **every** template value. Check the **preview** and the **charge** (*Meta bills this when it is sent*).
6. Click **Send and open chat**. It is yours.

WhatsApp only allows a **template** as the first message. Free typing starts once the customer replies. If the customer has asked **not to be contacted**, the system refuses to send.

### New email

1. If no mailbox is connected you will see **Open email settings**. The mailbox needs **IMAP** switched on.
2. Fill **Recipient email**, optional **Cc/Bcc**, **Subject**, **Message** (recipient, subject and message are required).
3. Click **Send**. It is yours.

**Messenger and Instagram: you cannot start these.** The customer must write first.

**Limits to protect the agency** (so a mistake cannot cost money): per person per hour — 15 new WhatsApp chats, 20 new emails, 60 template messages; per agency per day — 60 new WhatsApp chats, 100 new emails, 400 templates (resets at midnight, Sri Lanka time). If you reach one you are told when you can try again.

## 13. Who owns a conversation? Take it, give it, close it

Every chat has **one owner**. This stops two people answering the same customer.

**The chips in the chat header**

| Chip | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Assigned to you / Assigned to Name / Unassigned** | The owner. If you may assign chats, it is a drop-down. |
| *(no extra chip)* | **Assistant active** — normal. |
| **Staff action needed** (red) | The helper stopped. **A person must reply.** |
| **Assistant paused** | A person owns it; the helper stays silent. |
| **Closed** | Finished. A new message from the customer reopens it. |

**The ⋯ menu**

| Action | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Take control** | You become the owner; the helper stops. (Refused if a colleague already owns it.) |
| **Hand back to AI** | Lets the helper answer again. Only the owner or an administrator, and not while a review is open. |
| **Close conversation** | Moves it to *Closed*. Asks you to confirm. Nothing is deleted. |
| **Delete conversation** | **Administrators only.** Removes the whole chat **for good**. |

**Give a chat to a colleague** — use the owner drop-down in the header (or press **a**), then pick the person. They are notified, and the helper pauses. Choosing **Unassigned** on a person-owned chat sets it to **Staff action needed** so it does not vanish.

You cannot give a **closed** chat to anyone, and the new owner must be an **active** colleague who is allowed to use the Inbox. If two people change a chat at the same moment you will see *“Someone else changed this conversation just now. Refresh and try again.”* — just refresh.

**Automatic assignment** (if your administrator switched it on): new chats are given to someone automatically — first to the customer's usual person if they are available, then to the right specialist (group, visa, documents), then to whoever has the fewest chats. Nobody who is off shift, on leave or inactive is chosen.

## 14. Understand the customer panel (right side)

Open it with the panel button in the chat header.

**Not linked to a lead yet**
- **Possible existing lead found** — the system thinks this might be someone you already know and **nothing is linked yet**. Each suggestion shows name, lead number, phone, **Very likely** or **Possible**, and why. Click **Link conversation** only if you are sure. **Create separate lead** means “different people” (and it will not suggest the pair again).
- **Link or create lead** — links to the lead with the exact same phone number, or creates one. (Administrator and Marketing.)

**Linked to a lead**
At the top you see name, lead number and stage, language, phone, email and small **Open lead / Open booking / Open departure group** buttons. Below:

1. **Red review cards** — always on top (see §16).
2. **Recommended next action** — one suggested step (see §15).
3. Sections you can open and close:

| Section | What it shows and does |
|---|---|
| **Trip** (“3 of 5 details”) | What we know: package, travel period, contact, number of travellers, room. **Ask about …** puts the next missing question in your reply box — you edit and send it. |
| **Booking** | If none: **Select departure group**, then **Create booking**. If one exists: reference, status, travellers, and (if your role may see money) the **balance**. A confirmed booking offers **Operations handoff**. |
| **Follow-up** | Pick a date and time → **Schedule WhatsApp follow-up** (must be in the future; you become the follow-up owner). |
| **Copilot** | Copilot's reading of the chat. |
| **Turn into work** | **Create task or case** (§17). |
| **Conversation history** | When the chat started and every change of owner. |

> **Selecting a departure group only records interest. It does not hold seats.** Seats are held when the **booking** is created.

## 15. Use Copilot, your helper

Copilot reads each conversation in the background. **Nothing in the panel sends a message by itself.**

**Who gets Copilot buttons?** Administrator and Marketing. Operations, Finance and Visa do not. The CEO can read it but cannot send. Your administrator may also have switched it off or set it to “Observe only”. **The Inbox works fully without Copilot.**

**Copilot's status line is honest**

| It says | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Copilot is reading this conversation** | Still working. Carry on manually. |
| **Read by Copilot** | A reading exists. |
| **Keyword reading — “Keyword match, not the AI model”** | Simple rules read it. |
| **This reading may be out of date** | New messages arrived. Read them yourself. |
| **Did not read / could not read** | Skipped or failed. Work normally. |

**What it shows**
- **What they want** — e.g. *Asking for a price*, *Wants to book*, *Says they have paid*, *Complaint*, *Visa question*, *Looks like spam*.
- **Urgency** — Low / Normal / High / Critical.
- **How they feel** — Positive / Neutral / Concerned / Angry / Distressed.
- **Writing in** — English / Sinhala / Tamil.
- **Travel details** it found (journey, travellers, period, room, budget, departure city).
- **Flags** — e.g. *Asked for a refund*, *Mentioned a bank account we have not approved*.
- A percentage like **“73% sure”** or **“Only 45% sure. Check before relying on it.”**

Click **Why?** beside any fact to see **the customer's own words** it used, with a button to jump to that message. **Check the words, not just the percentage.**

**Wrong reading?** Pick the right choice in **Review this triage reading** and click **Record**. This improves your agency's accuracy figures; it does not change the chat.

**Recommended next action** — one card that picks the single most useful step, e.g. *Follow up on payment*, *Open a complaint case*, *Create a visa task*, *Request documents*, *Create quote*, or *Draft with Copilot*. A button may be greyed out with the reason in words (for example *“Find a matching departure with live price and seats before creating a quote.”*). It never does anything by itself.

**Draft with Copilot**
1. Click **Draft with Copilot** (or **Draft reply** in the “window closing” banner).
2. A suggested reply appears **in your box**.
3. **Read it. Edit it. Then send.**
4. Check: name, trip, dates, party, room, price and currency, what is included, **live seats**, tone, language, no open review, and **no promise** about payment, visa, discounts, refunds, bookings, health or religion.
5. It will refuse if the customer **opted out of contact**.

**The offer card (“Best departure”)** — when Copilot finds a matching trip it shows the fit (Strong / Good / Partial / Weak), date, price per person and room, seats left, total for the party, why it matches, what is included, things to be aware of, and other options.

| Button | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Draft reply** | Puts an editable reply in your box. |
| **Create quote** | Makes a **draft** quote on the lead. **Nothing is sent.** Uses live price, seats and inclusions; **no discount**. |
| **Open group** | Opens the departure page in a new tab. |
| **Ask follow-up / Ask for missing details** | Puts the next useful question in your box. |

If it turns red — **“Human review required… Do not confirm this departure to the customer.”** — the price or seats changed, or the trip is full. The Draft and Quote buttons are removed. The card also says *“Seats and prices are checked again each time you open this”*.

**What Copilot never does:** confirm seats or payment, promise a visa, give a discount, promise a refund, give medical advice or a religious ruling.

## 16. Red “Human review required” cards

A red card means **a person must look at something.** It sits at the top of the customer panel.

It shows a **headline**, maybe a **“Do not confirm yet”** badge (blocking), **Why**, and **What to do**.

**Three buttons**

| Button | Use when | Needs a note? |
|---|---|---|
| **I am on it** | You are handling it. Others see “Someone is on it”. | No |
| **Resolve** | You checked and dealt with it. | **Yes** — “What did you find?” |
| **Dismiss** | It is not a real problem. | **Yes** — “Why is this not a problem?” |

**While a blocking review is open** the system **refuses** to send words that confirm what it protects. Resolve the review with a note first, or rephrase without confirming.

**Every kind of review**

| The card says | Blocks? | What to do | Who can close it |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Customer says they paid, but no payment is recorded** | **Yes** | Do not confirm. Acknowledge the message only. Finance checks the bank and records it. | Finance, Admin |
| **A bank account was mentioned that is not approved** | **Yes** | Do not send or confirm details. Finance adds it if genuine, otherwise warn the customer. | Finance, Admin |
| **Customer is asking for a refund** | **Yes** | Do not promise or refuse. Say a colleague will come back. | Finance, Admin |
| **Customer is worried about fraud** | **Yes** | Reassure with facts you can stand behind. Never send bank details in chat. | Finance, Admin |
| **Someone may be ill / medical emergency** | **Yes** | A person answers **now**. No medical advice. Tell them to contact local emergency services, then involve Operations. | Admin, Marketing, Operations, Finance |
| **Customer sounds distressed or in trouble** | **Yes** | A person answers now, kindly and personally. | same |
| **A price we sent has changed** | No | Check the trip and tell the customer the current price. | same |
| **The trip cannot take the whole party** | No | Do not promise seats. Offer another departure or the waitlist. | same |
| **A passport may not be valid long enough** | No | Ask for a renewed passport. | same |
| **Customer sent an ID or financial document** | No | Handle with care; move it to the traveller's record; do not repeat contents. | same |
| **A traveller is a minor or needs assistance** | No | Check guardian and assistance arrangements first. | same |
| **Customer quoted a booking we cannot find** | No | Search by name and phone. Do not say a booking exists until found. | same |
| **Customer is making a complaint** | No | Answer personally. Do not close it yourself. Say who will follow up and when. | same |
| **Customer asks for a religious ruling** | No | Give none. Kindly point to a qualified scholar or the group's Ustaz. | same |

If your agency turned it on, a review can also open when a **reply target is missed**.

> The **CEO** and **Visa** roles cannot close reviews. **Marketing** and **Operations** cannot close the four *money* reviews (payment, bank, refund, fraud). If you cannot close one, leave a **note and mention Finance**.

## 17. Turn a chat into a booking, quote, task or case

### Make a booking

You need: a **linked lead**, a **selected departure group**, the lead's **phone number**, and enough on the lead (travellers, room). The system tells you exactly what is missing.

1. In **Booking**, click **Select departure group** and choose one (it shows dates and seats left).
2. Click **Create booking**. It starts as **Deposit pending** and **holds seats**.
3. Schedule a **Follow-up** for the deposit.

(Administrator and Marketing only.)

### Make a draft quote
Offer card (or next-action card) → **Create quote**. A **draft** is made on the lead. **Nothing is sent to the customer.** Follow your normal process in **Leads** to send the formal quote.

### Create a task or case (“Turn into work”)

Customer panel → **Turn into work** → **Create task or case**. (Administrator, Marketing, Operations.)

| Item | What is created | Needs first |
|---|---|---|
| Request documents | Task for Operations | A selected departure group |
| Create visa task | Task for the Visa team | A selected departure group |
| Follow up on payment | Task for Finance | A selected departure group |
| Rooming request | Task for Operations | A selected departure group |
| Transport requirement | Task for Operations | A selected departure group |
| Escalate to guide | Task for the group's guide | A selected departure group |
| Open complaint case | A support case | A traveller record |
| Create traveller profile | Traveller profile from the lead | A lead with no profile yet |
| Record family / mahram link | How two travellers are related | A booking with 2+ travellers |
| Hold seats | A time-limited **hold** (not a booking) | A lead, a group, a phone number, no booking yet |
| Recommend a package | Records a recommendation on the lead | A lead |
| Request post-trip feedback | Task to send a survey | A selected departure group |

**A two-step safety check protects you:**
1. Choose the item, fill the small form, add an optional **Note for the team**, click **Review**.
2. A sheet says **“Check before creating — Nothing has been created yet”** and shows exactly what will be made. Click **Create**, or **Cancel** to withdraw it.

If an item is greyed out the menu says why (for example *“Link this conversation to a lead first.”*).

### Hand a confirmed booking over to Operations
**Booking** → **Operations handoff** (Administrator and Marketing). The system builds a **snapshot**: customer and booking, what the customer expects, and open items. Operations acknowledges it on their own page, and the sheet then says **“Operations acknowledged this on <date>”**. It is a snapshot: later edits do not change what Sales handed over.

## 18. Clean up: close, spam, delete

| | **Close** | **Mark as spam** | **Delete** |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Use when** | The matter is finished | The chat is junk | You must erase everything (admins only) |
| **Goes to** | **Closed** | **Spam** | Gone |
| **Undo?** | Customer writing again reopens it | **Yes** — Spam queue → **Not spam** | **No. Never.** |
| **Deletes data?** | No | No | **Everything** in that chat |
| **Asks you to confirm?** | Yes (one chat). **No** (bulk). | Yes | Yes |
| **Who** | Admin, Marketing, Operations | same | **Administrator only** |

**Selecting many chats at once**
1. Click the **checklist** button (**Select conversations**) — tick boxes appear.
2. Tick your chats (**at most 50**). The bar says “3 selected”.
3. Choose **Give to…**, **Close**, or **Mark as spam** (**Not spam** inside the Spam queue).
4. A message says what happened, e.g. *“Closed 3 conversations. 1 was left alone.”*
5. **Cancel** stops selecting.

A chat is “left alone” when it cannot be changed — already closed, already has that owner, a closed chat being assigned, or (for spam) it has a **booking**, an **open review**, or its lead is spam. Bulk actions **never send messages, never confirm payments and never touch reviews.**

**Spam notes:** Spam stops Copilot reading the chat but does not delete anything. A chat whose **lead** is marked spam cannot be restored from the Inbox until the lead's stage is changed.

# PART 4 — REAL SITUATIONS

## 20. Scenario book

Find the situation, follow the steps.

### A. A new customer asks about Umrah on WhatsApp
1. Open **New enquiries** or **Needs a reply**; click the chat.
2. Look at the **banner** — is the reply window open?
3. In the customer panel, look for **Possible existing lead found**. If yes and you are sure, **Link conversation**. If it is a new person, **Link or create lead**.
4. Fill the **Trip** gaps with **Ask about …**.
5. When Copilot finds a trip, check the **offer card** (price, seats, “Be aware”).
6. **Draft with Copilot** → **read, edit**, send.
7. Add an **internal note** if a colleague must follow up.

### B. The customer asks “How much?”
1. Check the offer card is **not red**.
2. **Create quote** (a draft; nothing is sent).
3. Draft a reply with the price from the card. Edit and send.

### C. The customer asks for a discount
Copilot makes no discount promise and **Create quote** adds none. **Take control**, tell the customer a colleague will confirm, follow your agency's discount approval, and leave a note.

### D. “I have paid” with a payment slip
1. A red **blocking review** appears: *“The customer says they paid, but no payment is recorded.”* The slip shows **Not verified**.
2. **Send acknowledgement**, edit, send. **Do not write “payment received”.**
3. **Finance / Admin / CEO:** **Copy to Finance** and **Open Finance review**. **Marketing / Operations:** leave a **note and mention Finance**.
4. Finance checks the bank, records the payment in Finance, then **resolves the review with a note**.
5. **Only then** tell the customer the payment arrived.

### E. A passport arrives
See §10, “Passport photo or PDF”. Open the original → choose traveller → **Review fields** → **Save to traveller record** → **Save to Documents** → **Assign visa officer** if needed → resolve any passport review with a note.

### F. A voice note arrives
Press **Play** and listen to all of it. Treat any transcript as a hint. Repeat important numbers back **in writing** and ask the customer to confirm.

### G. The WhatsApp window is about to close
1. The banner turns red: **“Reply window closes in 0h 40m”**.
2. Click **Draft reply** in the banner (or reply yourself). Send **now**.
3. If it already closed: click **Choose approved template**, fill the values, check the preview and **charge**, **Send approved template**. When the customer replies, free typing works again.
(Owners also get an in-app reminder about two hours before a window closes on an unanswered customer.)

### H. A Messenger customer wrote 3 days ago and is now complaining
- If a **complaint / distressed / fraud / medical / refund** review is open **and a person owns the chat** and it has been under 7 days: the banner says **“Human support reply allowed…”**. Write the reply **yourself** (never Copilot).
- Otherwise you cannot reply. Add a note and wait, or use another channel the customer agreed to.

### I. An Instagram customer wants to send a PDF
Instagram carries **photos only**. Ask for a photo, or move to WhatsApp or email.

### J. The same person is on two apps
Open the chat that has no lead. Read **Possible existing lead found** and its reasons. **Very likely** is still only a suggestion. **Link conversation** only if you are sure; otherwise **Create separate lead**.

### K. Two colleagues open the same chat
You see *“Name is writing a reply. Please coordinate.”* Add a note (“Taking this”), set the **owner**, and remember it is a warning, not a lock.

### L. The customer is angry or distressed
1. A red review appears and the chat shows in **Urgent** or **Complaints**.
2. **Take control**, click **I am on it**.
3. Reply personally and kindly. Do not argue. Do not promise a refund.
4. **Open complaint case** (§17).
5. Resolve the review with a note when handled.

### M. The customer asks for a refund or to cancel
A **blocking** refund review opens for Finance. Say a colleague will come back — **do not promise or refuse.** Add a note and mention Finance. Only Finance or an Admin closes that review.

### N. The customer asks a religious question
Give no ruling. Kindly say a qualified scholar or the group's Ustaz can help.

### O. A customer is ready to book
Confirm the lead is linked and has a phone number → **Select departure group** → **Create booking** (*Deposit pending*, seats held) → schedule a deposit follow-up. For only a temporary reservation use **Hold seats** (§17).

### P. A confirmed booking goes to Operations
**Booking → Operations handoff.** Read the sheet. Operations acknowledges it.

### Q. You are going on leave or ending your shift
1. **Assigned to me** → **Select conversations** → tick your chats (max 50).
2. **Give to…** → choose the colleague. They get one notification: “<You> gave you N conversations.”
3. Or change one chat with its owner drop-down.
(If you cannot, an administrator can reassign in bulk.)

### R. A chat is waiting on you but you are off
A colleague or administrator reassigns it. Choosing **Unassigned** turns it into **Staff action needed** so it shows in **Unassigned**.

### S. Clearing junk
**Select conversations** → tick junk → **Mark as spam** → confirm. Wrong choice? **Spam** queue → **Not spam**.

### T. A message failed
Click **Retry** (safe — no duplicates). If it keeps failing, read the red reason: closed window, disconnected app, or a test agency.

### U. A file will not attach
Read the red message. Usual causes: wrong type (must be JPG/PNG/PDF/Word/Excel/PowerPoint), too big (photo > 5 MB, document > 10 MB), macros or scripts inside, Instagram + a document, or the upload dropped. Remove it and attach again.

### V. You need to send a brochure
**+** → **Choose from vault** → pick it → add a caption → send. (Not on Instagram.)

### W. You need a customer's old messages
Use **search** (name, phone, email, lead reference). It searches **all** conversations, closed ones too. Open the chat and scroll up.

### X. A closed chat came back
The customer wrote again. It returns to **Needs a reply** and keeps its previous owner. Normal.

### Y. A colleague asks you to look at a chat
They may send a link ending `?conversation=…`, or **mention** you in a note (you get a notification). Open it.

### Z. Daily wrap-up
Check **Assigned to me, Urgent, Payments, Overdue, Nearing deadline, Unassigned**. Nothing should be overdue under your name. Note unfinished matters. Close finished chats.

### AA. The customer says “I sent a video / my location” but you see nothing
Those types are not accepted. The customer was told automatically. Ask for text, an image, a document or a voice message.

### AB. You are not sure whether to trust Copilot
Click **Why?** and read the customer's own words. If it says “Only 45% sure” or “Keyword match”, treat it as a hint. When in doubt, ask the customer.

# PART 5 — THE RULES OF EACH APP

## 21. WhatsApp, Messenger, Instagram and Email: what you may send

The **banner above the reply box** always tells you what you may send right now. It uses words and an icon, never colour alone.

| App | Free typing | After 24 hours |
|---|---|---|
| **WhatsApp** | Within **24 hours** of the customer's last message | Only an **approved template** (may cost money) |
| **Messenger** | Within 24 hours | Only a **person-written reply on an active support case**, for up to **7 days**; otherwise **not at all** |
| **Instagram** | Within 24 hours | Same as Messenger |
| **Email** | Any time | Not applicable |

**What the banner can say**

| Banner | Meaning | What you do |
|---|---|---|
| **Reply window open — “allowed for 18h 20m”** | You can type freely. | Reply normally. |
| **Reply window closes in 1h 05m** (red under 2 hours) | About to shut. | Reply **now**. **Draft reply** is one click. |
| **Reply window closed — “Choose an approved template…”** (WhatsApp) | Free typing is refused. | **Choose approved template.** |
| **Human support reply allowed for 3d 4h** (Messenger/Instagram) | Only a person may reply. | Write it yourself. |
| **Sending unavailable** | Nothing can be sent. | Add a note; wait for the customer; or use another app they agreed to. |
| **Email messaging available** | No window. | Send any time. |

**Sending an approved WhatsApp template in an existing chat**
1. In the banner, click **Choose approved template**. (None? An administrator must sync them.)
2. Click a template. You see its **name**, **category** (Marketing, Utility, Authentication…) and **projected charge**.
3. Fill **every** “Template value”.
4. Check the **preview** and **charge**. If it says *“Projected charge unavailable”*, the price is **not known yet — it is not free.**
5. Click **Send approved template**. You become the owner.

**Messenger and Instagram**
- You **cannot start** them. Until the customer writes you can only add notes.
- After 24 hours there is **no template** to reopen them.
- The 7-day exception needs **all** of: a person (not Copilot) writes it, a person owns the chat, a complaint / distressed / fraud / medical / refund review is open, and fewer than 7 days have passed.

**Maximum message length:** WhatsApp 4,096 characters, Messenger 2,000, Instagram about 1,000 bytes, Email effectively unlimited. Very long messages are split automatically.

**Test agencies:** if your agency is a **test agency**, messages are **never** sent to real customers (*“This is a test agency…”*). In test setups, messages only go to approved test contacts. Production agencies are not affected.

### How much Copilot does — four levels

| Level | Name | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| **L0** | **Observe only** | Reads and measures. Staff get no drafts. |
| **L1** | **Draft replies** | Prepares drafts for staff to check and send. *(Default for a new agency.)* |
| **L2** | **Safe automatic replies** | Sends approved greetings, FAQ answers and qualifying questions — **only when the evidence allows**. Never on by default. |
| **L3** | **Lead intake assistant** | Collects dates, departure city, room type and passport readiness, then hands over **before** price or booking. |

The level actually used is the **lowest** of what the plan allows, what is set, and how safe the conversation is. Whatever the level, these are **always** for a person: confirming payments, promising visas, discounts, guaranteeing seats, changing bookings, unapproved bank details, refunds or free cancellation, religious rulings, health or safety advice, closing a complaint, and marketing broadcasts. If staff keep rejecting Copilot's drafts, the level **drops automatically**.

### A safe start for a new agency
1. Connect the apps and check one real message **each way**.

## 25. One-page cheat sheet

**Start:** Inbox icon → **Assigned to me** → **Urgent** → **Complaints** → **Payments** → **Overdue** → **Documents** → **Needs a reply** → **Unassigned**.

**Reply:** open chat → read banner + red cards → **Reply** tab → type → **Enter**.
**Note:** **Internal note** tab → type → **Mention staff** → **Add note**.
**New chat:** **+** → WhatsApp (template) or Email.
**Window closed (WhatsApp):** **Choose approved template**.
**Message failed:** **Retry** (safe).
**Not yours:** owner drop-down (**a**) → pick a colleague.
**Payment claim:** acknowledge only → Finance confirms → then tell the customer.
**Passport:** open original → check fields → Save to traveller → Save to Documents.
**Junk:** Select → Mark as spam (undo in Spam queue).
**Shortcuts:** `j` `k` `/` `r` `n` `a` `q` `e` `b` `g d` `Esc` `?`

**Never:** promise price/seat/discount/refund/visa/bank details unconfirmed · treat a slip as payment · repeat passport or bank details in chat · delete a chat unless sure · bulk-close unseen chats.

**When unsure:** add a note, mention a colleague, and ask.
